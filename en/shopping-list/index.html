Grocery list

A grocery list that comes straight from your recipes

Add a recipe and the ingredients are on your list. Throw in coffee and paper towels yourself, and take one list to the store.

How to make a grocery list from recipes

It works with any recipe in Matlyst, whether you saved it from TikTok, a website, or typed it in yourself.

  1. Open a recipe or your meal plan. Pick one recipe, or a day you've planned.
  2. Add it to the grocery list. The ingredients go on the list. If two recipes call for onions, you get one line for onions.
  3. Shop by aisle. Items are grouped by aisle, like Produce, Dairy & eggs, and Spices & sauces. Check them off as you go.

Need something that isn't in a recipe? Tap + and add it. Cooking for more people? Change the servings for a recipe right from the list.

The grocery list is free. To share a live list with your household, you need Matlyst Pro. You can always send the list as text from the share button.

Stop running back for the one thing you forgot

When the list comes from the recipe, you're not writing it from memory. The ingredients are there, in the amounts the recipe calls for.

A shared grocery list for the household

With Matlyst Pro, everyone in your household sees the same list. Whoever is at the store checks things off, and the list updates for everyone.

Plan first, shop once

Put the week's dinners in your meal plan, add the whole week to the grocery list, and do one grocery run instead of three.

After the store

Checked-off items can go straight into your pantry, so Matlyst knows what you have at home when it suggests dinners. The pantry is free for a limited number of items. Read more about the pantry.

Grocery list questions

Is there a grocery list app that adds ingredients from recipes?

Yes. In Matlyst, you add a recipe or a planned day to the grocery list, and the ingredients go on the list with matching items combined.

Is the Matlyst grocery list free?

Yes. Sharing a live list with your household takes Matlyst Pro.

Can I share my grocery list?

Yes. With Matlyst Pro, your household shares one live list. On the free plan, you can send the list as text to anyone.

Does the list sort items by aisle?

Yes. Items are grouped by aisle, like Produce, Meat, and Dairy & eggs.

Can I add items that aren't in a recipe?

Yes. Tap + and type what you need.
