Grocery list

A grocery list that comes straight from your recipes

Add a recipe and the ingredients are on your list. Throw in coffee and paper towels yourself, and take one list to the store.

Add a recipe, or a whole planned week, to your Matlyst grocery list and the ingredients land on one list. Matching items are combined on one line, the list is sorted by store section, and items already in your pantry are unselected when you add a recipe.

How to make a grocery list from recipes

  1. Open a recipe or your meal plan. Pick one recipe, or a day or week you have planned.
  2. Add it to the grocery list. The ingredients go on the list, with the amounts the recipe calls for.
  3. Shop by section. Items are grouped by store section, like produce, dairy, and spices. Check them off as you go.

It works with any recipe in Matlyst, whether you saved it from a website, TikTok, or Instagram, or typed it in yourself. You can also add things that aren’t in a recipe, like coffee and paper towels.

What happens when two recipes need the same thing

Say two recipes both need an onion. You get one line for onions, not two. Matlyst puts items with the same name on one line. When the units differ, like 1 cup in one recipe and 200 g in another, the amounts are shown together on that line, such as “1 cup · 200 g,” because it does not guess how to convert them. To tidy it up, tap the amount and enter a single amount and unit.

The list is sorted by store section, so you walk the store once instead of crossing it twice for the one thing you forgot.

Plan first, shop once

A grocery list is only as good as the plan behind it. Put the week’s dinners on the meal plan, add the whole week to the grocery list, and do one trip instead of three. If you want the bigger picture of saving, organizing, and cooking recipes, see the recipe organizer app.

Skip what you already have

Items already in your pantry are unselected when you add a recipe to the list, and marked “In your pantry.” That’s a reminder, not a rule: you see what you already have before you buy a second jar. Check the pantry before you shop, and update it when you cook.

Share the list with your household

A Pro member can create a household for up to 6 people, and joining is free. The grocery list, the weekly menu, and the pantry are shared and update live for everyone. One person plans, another shops, and the person at the store checks things off for everyone.

Three situations, and what to do

Scenarios marked “Say you …” are examples we made up to show how it works. They are not customer stories.

Say you’re planning five dinners for a family of four

Situation
One budget, one trip to the store.
What goes wrong
Two recipes both need an onion and you buy three.
What to do
Plan the week first, then shop from one merged list.
In Matlyst
Add the recipes to the weekly menu, add the week to the grocery list, and shop by section.

Say your partner shops on the way home

Situation
You planned the week, and they are at the store.
What goes wrong
You text a list and half of it gets lost in the thread.
What to do
Share one live list.
In Matlyst
With a Pro household, the list is shared and updates for both of you. Joining is free.

Say you want to cook a recipe for seven, not four

Situation
Guests are coming.
What goes wrong
You scale the recipe in your head and under-buy.
What to do
Scale the servings first, then add the recipe to the list. Scaling is linear, so for baking, check eggs and leavening yourself.
In Matlyst
Change the servings, from 1 to 20 in total, then add it to the grocery list.

What the grocery list doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t order groceries. It’s a list, not a store.
  • It doesn’t convert units for you. Same units are added together, and different units appear as separate lines.
  • It can misread a recipe. Matlyst uses AI to read recipes from posts and photos, so check amounts before you rely on the list.

Grocery list questions

Is there a grocery list app that adds ingredients from recipes?

Yes. In Matlyst, you add a recipe or a planned day to the grocery list, and the ingredients go on the list with matching items combined.

Is the Matlyst grocery list free?

Yes, the grocery list is free. Creating a household to share a live list takes Matlyst Pro, and joining one is free.

Can I share my grocery list with my partner?

Yes. A Pro member creates a household for up to 6 people. The grocery list, weekly menu, and pantry update live for everyone, and joining is free.

Does the list sort items by store section?

Yes. Items are grouped by store section, like produce, meat, and dairy.

What happens when two recipes need the same ingredient?

Matlyst puts items with the same name on one line. When the units differ, the amounts are shown together on that line, like “1 cup · 200 g.”

Can I add items that aren’t in a recipe?

Yes. Tap + and type what you need.
