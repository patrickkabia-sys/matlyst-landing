Fridge storage times

How long does food last in the fridge?

Keep the fridge at 40°F (4°C) or below. The federal chart on this page assumes that temperature. Every time below comes from a named source.

What the federal sources say

FoodSafety.gov (2023) bases its refrigerator times on a fridge set at 40°F (4°C) or below.

USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers can be kept in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days.

USDA FSIS (2023) says never to leave food out of refrigeration over 2 hours, or 1 hour when the temperature is above 90°F (32°C).

Questions about fridge times

What temperature should a refrigerator be?

FoodSafety.gov (2023) bases its refrigerator times on 40°F (4°C) or below, and FDA (2023) says the same.

How long can food sit out before it goes in the fridge?

USDA FSIS (2023) says never more than 2 hours, or 1 hour when the temperature is above 90°F (32°C).
