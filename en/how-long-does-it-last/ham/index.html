Fridge storage times

How long does ham last in the fridge?

A whole cooked ham that was wrapped at the store lasts 1 week in the refrigerator. Slices, halves, and spiral cuts last 3 to 5 days, according to FoodSafety.gov (2023).

What the federal sources say

FoodSafety.gov (2023) has a separate block for ham, with a line for each kind. Fresh, uncured, uncooked ham lasts 3 to 5 days.

The chart also lists cured, vacuum-sealed, canned, country, and prosciutto-style hams. Some of those lines give the time as a "use by" date, so this page leaves them out.

USDA FSIS (2023) says never to leave food out of refrigeration over 2 hours. When the temperature is above 90°F (32°C), the limit is 1 hour.

FoodSafety.gov (2023) bases its refrigerator times on a fridge set at 40°F (4°C) or below.

How long it lasts

FoodSafety.gov (2023) lists these times for the refrigerator:

FoodTime in the fridge
Fresh, uncured, uncooked ham3 to 5 days
Cooked ham, store-wrapped, whole1 week
Cooked ham, store-wrapped, slices, half, or spiral cut3 to 5 days

Source: FoodSafety.gov (2023), “Cold Food Storage Chart”. Accessed November 21, 2026.

Questions about ham

Does a spiral-cut ham last as long as a whole ham?

No. The chart gives 3 to 5 days for slices, half, and spiral cut, and 1 week for a whole cooked ham.

Can you freeze ham?

FoodSafety.gov (2023) lists freezer times for each kind. See can you freeze ham.
