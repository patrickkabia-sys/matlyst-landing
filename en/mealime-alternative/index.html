What happened to Mealime

This is what Mealime says on its own closing page.

  • Mealime was discontinued on October 21, 2026.
  • Mealime’s recipes and meal-planning tools are moving to Meals Hub, which is available in Albertsons Companies grocery apps and on Albertsons store websites. Mealime says Meals Hub comes from the same core team and is free, including the recipes that used to be Pro.
  • Paid subscriptions were wound down, and Pro subscribers were not charged any more subscription fees before the shutdown.
  • Your account information does not transfer automatically. Some preferences have to be set up again in Meals Hub.
  • Mealime says all personal data will be deleted when the app shuts down.

If you shop at an Albertsons Companies store, Meals Hub is the route Mealime itself points to, and it may be the simplest thing to try first. The rest of this page is for people who want their recipes in a place they control.

Bring a Mealime recipe into Matlyst by link

Mealime’s published recipes are still open on mealime.com/recipes, where you can browse and search. Each recipe has its own page, and you can bring it into Matlyst one at a time.

  1. Find the recipe on mealime.com/recipes and open it.
  2. Share the page to Matlyst from the Share Sheet. Or copy the link, open Add recipe in Matlyst, choose Paste a link, and paste it.
  3. Check the draft before you save it. Look over the amounts and ingredient names, and fix anything that needs fixing.

We tested this on November 20, 2026, with two recipes from mealime.com. Both came in with ingredients, steps, and a photo, in about 7 and 12 seconds. One had 12 ingredients and 7 steps, the other 15 ingredients and 14 steps. The number of servings didn’t come along, so set it in the draft before you save.

Mealime has shut down, and we don’t control its website. If Mealime takes those pages down, this stops working, so it’s worth saving the recipes you want soon.

What Matlyst can and can’t bring over

There’s no bulk Mealime import. Matlyst doesn’t connect to Mealime, it can’t read a Mealime account, and it never asks for your Mealime login. Matlyst is not affiliated with Mealime or Albertsons, and their names belong to their owners.

Link import only reaches recipes that Mealime published. Recipes you created yourself in Mealime had no public page, so a link can’t reach them. Start with the recipes you cook most, not the whole collection, since each one comes over by hand.

A photo or a screenshot

If you have one of your own recipes on a screen, in a screenshot, or on a printout, choose Take a photo in Add recipe. Pick the photo or screenshot from your library, or photograph the page. If a recipe runs over several screenshots, add them all, in order. Photo import is a Matlyst Pro feature. Image quality affects the result, and Matlyst can misread quantities, so compare the draft with the original.

Type it in

If you can copy the text, or you know the recipe by heart, choose Type it in, then paste or type the recipe. Matlyst sorts it into ingredients and steps.

An export from a third-party tool

If you saved your recipes with a third-party export tool, Matlyst can’t read that file. Open the recipes in the tool, copy the text of each one, and paste it under Type it in. Or use the mealime.com links to the original recipes, where they exist.

Imports can make mistakes, because AI reads the recipe. Look over amounts and ingredient names before you cook. Amounts are metric by default. Switch to Units: US in Settings and Matlyst keeps the cups, ounces, and degrees Fahrenheit the source used. It doesn’t convert metric recipes into cups for you.

Can you export your recipes from Mealime?

Mealime doesn’t offer an official export, and its closing page doesn’t mention one. On November 20, 2026, the Mealime web app was showing “Offline for Maintenance.” The published recipes on mealime.com/recipes were still online.

So the recipes you can still reach are the published ones, and those you can bring over by link. A recipe you created yourself is only safe if you already have a screenshot or a copy. Mealime says personal data will be deleted at shutdown.

Grocery list

Add one recipe, a day, or the whole week to the grocery list. Matching items go on one line, and the list is grouped by store section. When units differ, such as 1 cup in one recipe and 200 g in another, the amounts show together on that line, because Matlyst doesn’t guess how to convert them. Read more about the grocery list.

What it costs

Matlyst is free to start, with 5 imports per calendar month. Folders, search, the meal plan, and the grocery list are free. Photo import, Ask Matlyst, and creating a household are part of Matlyst Pro, and joining a household is free. The current Pro options are shown in the app before you buy.

You can also export your recipes at any time, for free, as a Matlyst file (a zip with folders and photos) or as HTML, so the next move is easier than this one.

Mealime questions

What happened to Mealime?

According to its closing page, Mealime was discontinued on October 21, 2026, and its recipes and meal-planning tools are moving to Meals Hub in Albertsons Companies store apps.

Can I import my Mealime recipes into Matlyst?

One at a time, by link, if Mealime published them on mealime.com/recipes. We tested it on November 20, 2026. There’s no bulk import, and Matlyst doesn’t connect to Mealime accounts. Recipes you created yourself have no public page, so save those from a photo or screenshot (Matlyst Pro) or type them in.

Will the link import keep working?

We can’t promise it. It depends on Mealime keeping its recipe pages online, so save the recipes you want soon.

Why is the number of servings missing?

In our test, the servings didn’t come along from mealime.com. Set the number of servings in the draft before you save the recipe.

Can I export my recipes from Mealime?

Mealime doesn’t offer an official export, and its closing page doesn’t mention one. Published recipes are still on mealime.com/recipes, and you can bring those over by link.

Does Matlyst ask for my Mealime login?

No. Matlyst never connects to your Mealime account or asks for your Mealime password.

Can I save a recipe I made myself in Mealime?

Only from a copy you already have. With Matlyst Pro, take a photo or screenshot of it in Add recipe. Without Pro, choose Type it in and paste or type the recipe. Look over the draft against the original.

Does Matlyst make a meal plan for me?

No. You choose the recipes and put them on the days you want. Matlyst then sends the ingredients to one grocery list.

Does Matlyst have the same recipes Mealime had?

No. Matlyst works with the recipes you save yourself. Mealime says its recipes continue in Meals Hub.

Is Matlyst free?

Matlyst is free to start, with 5 imports per calendar month. The meal plan and the grocery list are free. Photo import, Ask Matlyst, and creating a household are part of Matlyst Pro.

Is Meals Hub an option?

Mealime’s closing page points to Meals Hub, which it says is free and available in Albertsons Companies apps. If you shop there, it may be the easiest thing to try. Matlyst is for people who want to keep their own recipes in one place.
