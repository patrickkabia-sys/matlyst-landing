Questions and answers

How much does Matlyst cost?

The app is free, and you can save 5 recipes from links every month without paying. Pro removes that limit and lets you save from photos too. You’ll see the price in the app before you buy.

Where can I save recipes from?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, recipe websites, and food blogs. Tap Share on the post or page and choose Matlyst. The recipe needs to be written out in the post, the video description, or on the page. With Pro, you can also snap a photo of a recipe in a cookbook.

Can I move my recipes over from Paprika?

Yes. Export your recipes from Paprika, then choose the export file in Matlyst.

Are recipes in other languages translated?

Yes. The ingredients and steps are translated into the language you use in the app. Always check the amounts against the original.

Can my household share the meal plan and grocery list?

Yes. With Pro, you can invite your household, up to 6 people. It’s free for the people you invite.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Matlyst is available for iPhone, iPad, and Android.
