Recipe import

Import recipes from social media, websites and apps

Found something you want to cook? Share the link, video, photo, text or export file with Matlyst and turn it into a recipe with organised ingredients and instructions.

Save your first recipe

Before your first import, open Matlyst once and accept the AI notice. Matlyst uses AI to read and translate recipes.

  1. Find the recipe. Open the post, video or web page with the recipe on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or a food blog.
  2. Share it to Matlyst. Tap Share and choose Matlyst. Pick a folder if you like, then tap Save. Matlyst fetches the recipe in the background.
  3. Check it over. Open the recipe in Matlyst and look over the ingredients and method before you start cooking.

Prefer to paste? Copy the link, open Add recipe in Matlyst and choose Paste a link. Tap Get recipe, then Save recipe.

Matlyst can only read what’s publicly visible in a post. If a post is private, or the recipe isn’t in the caption, copy the text and paste it under Type it in instead.

Ways to import a recipe

  • Instagram: share a public post or reel with Matlyst. Read the guide to importing recipes from Instagram.
  • TikTok: share a public video or paste its link. Read the guide to importing recipes from TikTok.
  • YouTube: share a video, Short or public playlist. A playlist opens a picker so you can choose which videos to import.
  • Recipe websites: share the page from your browser or paste its address from a food blog or recipe site.
  • Pinterest: open the original recipe website linked by the pin, then share or paste that page. A pin does not always contain the full recipe.
  • A photo: scan a cookbook page, magazine, handwritten card or screenshot. You can use more than one image for a multi-page recipe. Photo import needs Matlyst Pro. Read the guide to importing recipes from photos.
  • Text: paste or type a recipe straight into the app.
  • Paprika: export your collection and bring the file into Matlyst. Follow the guide to importing recipes from Paprika.

In English, whatever the original language

Matlyst translates the ingredients and method into your app’s language, so with the app in English, every recipe arrives in English. It uses metric units unless you switch to US units in Settings. A link to the source stays with the recipe, so you can always find your way back to the person who made it.

Put your recipes to work

Sort your recipes into folders and find them again when it’s time to decide what to cook. Add a recipe to your meal plan, send its ingredients to your shopping list, or open Ask Matlyst on the recipe to ask about ingredient swaps, allergens, preparation and serving ideas.

Questions and answers

How do I save a recipe from Instagram or TikTok?

Tap Share on the post or video and choose Matlyst. Pick a folder if you like and tap Save. The recipe is ready in Matlyst a few seconds later.

Can I import a recipe from YouTube?

Yes. Share a YouTube video or Short with Matlyst, or paste its link. You can also share a public playlist and choose which videos to import. The recipe needs to be available in the video description or other text Matlyst can access.

Can I import recipes from Pinterest?

Open the original recipe website linked by the Pinterest pin, then share or paste that page in Matlyst. A pin does not always contain the complete recipe.

Can I move recipes from Paprika to Matlyst?

Yes. Export your recipes from Paprika, open the import area in Matlyst and choose the export file. Matlyst supports Paprika recipe files and compatible ZIP exports.

Are recipes in other languages translated?

Yes. The ingredients and method are translated into your app language. Metric units are the default, and you can switch to US units in Settings.

Can I save a handwritten recipe?

Yes, with Matlyst Pro. Take a photo and Matlyst reads the text and adds it, just like a recipe from the web.

What does it cost?

You get 5 free link imports a month. Matlyst Pro removes that limit and adds photo import. The current price and terms are shown in the app before you buy.
