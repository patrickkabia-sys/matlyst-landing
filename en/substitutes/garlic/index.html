Ingredient substitutes › Garlic

What can I use instead of a garlic clove?

Three extension sources agree: a small clove of garlic is 1/8 teaspoon of garlic powder.

Colorado State Extension, Nebraska Extension, and Texas A&M AgriLife Extension all say 1/8 teaspoon of garlic powder for 1 small clove of garlic. Colorado State Extension also says instant minced garlic works in that amount.

Replacements for one small clove

Garlic powder

Ratio
1/8 teaspoon (Colorado State Extension, Nebraska Extension, Texas A&M AgriLife Extension).
What changes
The sources do not describe flavor.

Instant minced garlic

Ratio
Colorado State Extension: 1/8 teaspoon, the same as the powder.
What changes
The source gives no more detail.

Garlic salt

Ratio
Colorado State Extension: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon of garlic salt.
What changes
Reduce the amount of salt called for in the recipe.

A note on small cloves

The ratio in all three sources is for a small clove. Cloves vary a lot in size, so a big clove may need more powder. The sources do not say how much more.

Questions and answers

How much garlic powder equals one clove?

1/8 teaspoon, according to Colorado State Extension, Nebraska Extension, and AgriLife Extension.

Can I use garlic salt?

Colorado State Extension: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon of garlic salt, and use less salt in the recipe.

What about a large clove?

The sources give the ratio for a small clove only.
