Freezer storage times

Can you freeze hard-boiled eggs?

No. FoodSafety.gov (2023) marks hard-cooked eggs "do not freeze." What the chart gives is the fridge time: 1 week.

What the federal sources say

The chart row for hard-cooked eggs has a refrigerator time and the words "Do not freeze" in the freezer column.

USDA FSIS (2024) says hard-cooked eggs should be refrigerated within two hours of cooking and used within a week.

FoodSafety.gov (2023) writes its freezer times for a freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or below.

Freezer times

FoodSafety.gov (2023) lists these times for the freezer:

FoodTime in the freezerTime in the fridge
Hard-cooked eggsDo not freeze1 week

Source: FoodSafety.gov (2023), “Cold Food Storage Chart”. Read November 21, 2026.
