How much per person

How much food per person?

Pick a food, enter how many adults and kids are eating, and get the total amount. Every number comes from a source.

adults

4 lb in total

Whole turkey: 4 adults need 4 lb (1.8 kg).

Short answer: USDA FSIS (2010) says to allow 1 lb (455 g) of turkey per person. Iowa State Extension says plan for 1 to 1.5 pounds of food per guest at a party. Each page has the numbers for one food, with the source.

Source: USDA FSIS (2010), “Let's Talk Turkey: A Consumer Guide to Safely Roasting a Turkey”.

How we work out the amounts

Every number on these pages comes from a source, and the source is named with a link and a year on the page. We do not make up our own numbers for what is enough. All we do is multiply the source's amount per person by the number of people.

Kids: each kid counts as half an adult in the calculator, because Iowa State Extension lists half the adult amount per child (Iowa State Extension, 2025).

US measures come first and metric is in parentheses. The parentheses are our conversion of the source's figure, rounded. The figure in the source is always the US one.

Where sources disagree, the page shows both numbers and says which one the calculator uses. That is usually the first one listed.

Questions about amounts per person

How much turkey per person?

USDA FSIS says to allow 1 lb (455 g) per person. The National Turkey Federation says at least 1 pound of uncooked turkey, and 1.25 to 1.5 pounds for seconds or leftovers.

Do kids count as a whole person?

The calculator counts each kid as half an adult, because Iowa State Extension lists half the adult amount per child.

Where do the numbers come from?

From USDA, university extension services, the National Turkey Federation, and other named sources. Every page has the sources with a link and a year.

Why do some pages show more than one number for the same food?

Sources do not always agree. The page shows both and says which one the calculator uses.
