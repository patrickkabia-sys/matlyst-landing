How much per person

How much mashed potatoes per person?

For mashed potatoes, Whole Foods Market (2026) says to buy 1/2 lb (225 g) of potatoes per guest. Ready to serve, it lists 3/4 cup (180 mL) of potatoes or starch per person.

Raw potatoes for mashed potatoes: 4 adults need 2 lb (905 g).

Short answer: Buy 1/2 lb (225 g) of raw potatoes per person. Ready to serve, plan on 3/4 cup (180 mL) each. Iowa State Extension lists 5 oz (140 g) of potatoes per serving.

Source: Whole Foods Market (2026), “Holiday Servings Calculator”.

What the sources say

Three sources give three kinds of number: raw weight, cooked volume, and a serving weight. The calculator starts with the raw weight.

Raw weight and what you get

Idaho Potato Commission (2026) says that 100 servings of 1/2 cup need 25 pounds of potatoes. The calculator divides that by 100 to give the amount for one half-cup serving.

Whole Foods Market lists a bigger serving of potatoes at a holiday dinner.

Questions about mashed potatoes

How many pounds of potatoes for 12 people?

Using Whole Foods Market (2026), 12 adults need 6 lb (2.7 kg) of raw potatoes for mashed potatoes.

How many cups of mashed potatoes for 8 people?

Whole Foods Market (2026) lists 3/4 cup (180 mL) per person, so 6 cups (1.4 L) for 8 adults.

Do the sources agree?

They measure different things: raw pounds, cooked cups, and a serving weight. The table shows each one.
