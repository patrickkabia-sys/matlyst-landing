How much per person

How much food per person for Thanksgiving dinner?

Whole Foods Market (2026) lists an amount per person for every dish on the table. Pick a dish in the calculator, enter your adults and kids, and you get the whole amount.

adults
kids, half portion
5 lb in total

Turkey: 4 adults need 5 lb (2.3 kg).

Short answer: Per person: 1 1/4 lb (565 g) of turkey, 3/4 cup (180 mL) of stuffing, 3/4 cup (180 mL) of potatoes, 1 cup (240 mL) of vegetables, and 1/2 cup (120 mL) of gravy. Source: Whole Foods Market (2026).

Source: Whole Foods Market (2026), “Holiday Servings Calculator”.

Questions about thanksgiving dinner

How much food for 8 people?

Using Whole Foods Market (2026), 8 adults need 10 lb (4.5 kg) of turkey, 6 cups (1.4 L) of stuffing, 6 cups (1.4 L) of potatoes, and 4 cups (960 mL) of gravy.

How many pounds of potatoes for mashed potatoes?

Whole Foods Market (2026) says 1/2 lb (225 g) per guest, so 5 lb (2.3 kg) for 10 adults.

How many pies?

Whole Foods Market (2026) lists 1 slice of a 9 inch pie per person.
