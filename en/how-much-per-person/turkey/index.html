How much per person

How much turkey per person?

Allow 1 lb (455 g) of turkey per person, says USDA FSIS (2010). For seconds or leftovers, National Turkey Federation (2019) says 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 lb (565 g to 680 g).

Turkey for seconds and extras

National Turkey Federation (2019) says that for the guest who is always reaching for seconds, or if you want to count on leftovers, 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 lb (565 g to 680 g) per person is a safe bet.

That is the second row of the calculator. Pick it from the menu above the numbers.

A bigger group

National Turkey Federation (2019) says that for a group that needs more than 15 lb (6.8 kg) of turkey, you may want to use two smaller birds to help with even cooking.

The same page suggests a bone-in or boneless breast next to a whole bird, to add white meat for a group that falls in between.

Questions about turkey

How many pounds of turkey for 10 people?

With the USDA figure of 1 lb (455 g) per person, 10 adults need 10 lb (4.5 kg). For seconds or leftovers, 12 1/2 to 15 lb (5.7 kg to 6.8 kg).

Does the pound figure include the bone?

Whole Foods Market (2026) says its 1 1/4 lb (565 g) per person includes bone weight. USDA FSIS (2010) gives its figure for a whole turkey.

Do kids count?

The calculator counts each kid as half an adult, because Iowa State Extension lists half the adult amount per child.
