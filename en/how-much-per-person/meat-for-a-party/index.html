How much per person

How much meat per person for a party?

At a party, Iowa State Extension lists 6 to 8 oz (170 g to 225 g) of protein per person, or 1.5 sandwiches. In all, plan for 1 to 1 1/2 lb (455 g to 680 g) of food per guest.

Questions about meat for a party

How much meat for 50 people?

For sandwiches, Iowa State Extension (2026) lists 20 to 25 lb (9.1 kg to 11.3 kg) per 50 guests of raw meat. For sliders, 10 to 12 1/2 lb (4.5 kg to 5.7 kg) per 50 guests.

How much food per guest in total?

1 to 1 1/2 lb (455 g to 680 g), says Iowa State Extension (2026).

What if I do not know how many will come?

The same source suggests planning for 60% of the guests invited at an open house.
