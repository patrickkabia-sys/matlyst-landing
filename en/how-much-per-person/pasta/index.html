How much per person

How much pasta per person?

Iowa State Extension, citing the USDA, says 2 oz (55 g) of dry pasta per person. That gives about 1 cup (240 mL) of cooked pasta.

Dry pasta: 4 adults need 8 oz (225 g).

Short answer: 2 oz (55 g) of dry pasta per person, which is about 1 cup (240 mL) cooked. A pound of dry pasta is about right for 8 people.

Source: Iowa State Extension (2021), “Celebrating Spaghetti”.

What the sources say

Two sources describe the same serving from two sides: dry weight and cooked volume.

Dry ounces to cups

Nebraska Extension (2026) cites the National Pasta Association: 8 oz (225 g) of uncooked elbow macaroni, shells, rotini, spaghetti, linguine, and similar shapes all give about 4 cups (960 mL) of cooked pasta. For egg noodles, 8 oz (225 g) uncooked gives about 2 1/2 cups (600 mL) cooked.

Questions about pasta

How much pasta for 8 people?

Iowa State Extension (2021) says a pound is about right. By the ounce, 1 lb (455 g).

How many cups of cooked pasta per person?

About 1 cup (240 mL), according to Iowa State Extension (2021).

Is the serving the same for every shape?

The sources give 2 oz (55 g) dry, and Iowa State Extension says how to measure it by shape.
