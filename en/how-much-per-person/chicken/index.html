How much per person

How much chicken per person?

Iowa State Extension lists 3/4 to 1 lb (340 g to 455 g) per serving for bone-in meats such as poultry. At a party, its table lists 6 to 8 oz (170 g to 225 g) of protein per person.

Bone-in chicken: 4 adults need 3 to 4 lb (1.4 kg to 1.8 kg).

Short answer: 3/4 to 1 lb (340 g to 455 g) of bone-in chicken per person for a sit-down meal, says Iowa State Extension (2025). For a party, Iowa State Extension (2026) lists 6 to 8 oz (170 g to 225 g) of any protein per person.

Source: Iowa State Extension (2025), “Tackling the Holiday Meat Puzzle”.

What the sources say

We found no extension source that names chicken by itself. Iowa State Extension groups poultry with other bony meats, and lists party protein without naming a meat. The page says exactly that.

Where the bone-in figure comes from

Iowa State Extension (2025) sorts meats by how much bone they have. Poultry sits with short ribs, spare ribs, shanks, and hocks, at 3/4 to 1 lb (340 g to 455 g) per serving. Boneless cuts and ground meat get a smaller figure.

Questions about chicken

How much chicken for 10 adults?

For a sit-down meal, 7 1/2 to 10 lb (3.4 kg to 4.5 kg) of bone-in chicken, using Iowa State Extension (2025).

How much chicken for a party?

Iowa State Extension (2026) lists 6 to 8 oz (170 g to 225 g) of protein per person. For 10 adults that is 3 3/4 to 5 lb (1.7 kg to 2.3 kg).

Is this the cooked or raw weight?

The sources say per serving or per person and do not state whether the weight is cooked or raw.
