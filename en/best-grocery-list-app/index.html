Best grocery list apps: six apps compared

We make Matlyst, so we have a stake in this. That’s why every fact here has a source and a date. Here are six grocery list apps you can get in the US, compared on what the apps say about themselves.

If you just want a shared list for free, Bring! and AnyList say making and sharing lists is free, and Listonic and OurGroceries are free list apps built for sharing. If you want deals from nearby stores on the list, Bring! shows them. If you want to order online, AnyList works with Walmart, Instacart, Kroger, and more. If you want the list made from your recipes and meal plan, look at Matlyst or AnyList.

The short answer: pick by what you need

  • You want one shared list, for free: Bring! and AnyList say making and sharing lists is free. Listonic and OurGroceries are free to use, and both share lists across the household.
  • You want the list made from recipes: Matlyst builds it from your saved recipes and meal plan. AnyList does too, and its meal plan takes AnyList Complete. OurGroceries adds a recipe’s ingredients in one tap.
  • You want to order groceries online: AnyList lets you shop online at Walmart, Instacart, Kroger, and more.
  • You want deals while you write the list: Bring! shows offers from stores near you.
  • You keep an eye on the total: Listonic and Out of Milk add up the list, and AnyList does with AnyList Complete.
  • You want a pantry list too: Matlyst and Out of Milk both have one.

Questions and answers

What is the best grocery list app?

It depends on how you shop. For a free shared list, look at Bring!, Listonic, or OurGroceries. To order online, look at AnyList. If the list should come from your recipes, look at Matlyst or AnyList.

Which grocery list app can my family share?

All of these say you can share: AnyList, Bring!, Listonic, OurGroceries, and Out of Milk (by text or email). In Matlyst, sharing with your household takes Matlyst Pro.

Is there a free grocery list app?

Yes. Every app on this page is free to download. Bring!, Listonic, and OurGroceries say their lists are free, and the grocery list in Matlyst is free. Some of the free apps show ads.

Which grocery list apps sort by aisle?

AnyList, Bring!, Listonic, and OurGroceries say they sort the list by category or aisle, and AnyList and Bring! let you match it to your store. Out of Milk groups items into categories, and Matlyst groups the list by store section.
