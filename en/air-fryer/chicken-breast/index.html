Air fryer chicken breast: time and temperature

The sources do not agree on chicken breast. Cosori, Mississippi State Extension, and Philips give three different temperatures. Here they are side by side, with the year of each.

Cosori (2026) lists boneless breasts at 390°F (199°C) for 15 to 22 minutes. Mississippi State Extension (2024) gives 380°F (193°C) for 12 minutes, and Philips (2013) gives 360°F (182°C) for 10 to 15 minutes. Treat the times as a place to start. USDA FSIS (2025) sets the safe minimum for poultry at 165°F (74°C), measured with a thermometer.

Philips (2013) is the Philips US Airfryer recipe book, so it is the oldest source here.

Why the numbers differ

Cosori (2026) ties its times to the size of the breast and marks each row for flipping. A thin-cut breast is listed at 10 to 18 minutes, and a bone-in breast at 24 to 34 minutes.

Mississippi State Extension (2024) and Philips (2013) do not say how heavy the breast is, so their times are harder to compare. Mississippi State Extension (2024) also says you may need to adjust times and temperatures for the size of the food and for your air fryer.

Thin cuts finish sooner than a chart says

Cosori (2026) says chicken cutlets and fish fillets under three quarters of an inch finish faster than expected, so check them sooner. It also says a timer is a cue to check, not a guarantee.

Core temperature: the thermometer decides, not the clock

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) as the minimum internal temperature for all poultry, including breasts, measured with a food thermometer.

The times above only tell you when to start checking. The temperature decides, not the clock.

Questions about air fryer chicken breast

Do I flip chicken breast in the air fryer?

Cosori (2026) marks all three chicken breast rows for flipping. Philips (2013) does not say.

What temperature is chicken breast done at?

USDA FSIS (2025) says 165°F (74°C) for all poultry. Cosori says the internal temperature is what confirms the food is done, not the time.

How long does frozen chicken breast take in the air fryer?

We did not find a source with air fryer times for raw frozen chicken breast, so we give none.
