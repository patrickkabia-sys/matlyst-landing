Air fryer chicken wings: time and temperature

Five sources give five different answers for chicken wings, in both temperature and time. Here they are side by side.

Cosori (2026) lists wings at 400°F (204°C) for 16 to 22 minutes. KitchenAid (2026) says 425°F (218°C) for 18 to 20 minutes, and Instant Pot (2025) says 360°F (182°C) for 20 to 30 minutes. Treat the times as a place to start. USDA FSIS (2025) sets the safe minimum for poultry at 165°F (74°C).

Why five sources give five answers

The temperature goes from 360°F (182°C) at Instant Pot (2025) and 360°F (182°C) at Philips (2013) up to 425°F (218°C) at KitchenAid (2026). Cosori (2026) and Mississippi State Extension (2024) both list a two-pound batch at 400°F (204°C), but their times are 16 to 22 minutes and 12 minutes.

That gap is a reminder that wattage, basket size, and how many wings you load change the result. Cosori (2026) says a full basket reduces airflow and makes the time longer.

Shake the basket and watch the fat

Cosori (2026) marks wings for shaking. Philips (2013) says to shake small items such as fries and wings once or twice during the time, and to empty the fat from the bottom now and then when you cook high-fat foods such as wings, to avoid excess smoke.

Core temperature: the thermometer decides, not the clock

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) as the minimum internal temperature for all poultry, wings included.

The times above only tell you when to start checking. The temperature decides, not the clock.

Questions about air fryer chicken wings

What temperature should I air fry chicken wings at?

It depends on the source: 360°F (182°C) at Instant Pot (2025), 400°F (204°C) at Cosori (2026), and 425°F (218°C) at KitchenAid (2026).

How long do chicken wings take?

Mississippi State Extension (2024) says 12 minutes for two pounds, and Instant Pot (2025) says 20 to 30 minutes. The thermometer decides: 165°F (74°C) for poultry, says USDA FSIS (2025).

Do I need to preheat?

Cosori (2026) says a preheat of 3 minutes is enough for most foods.
