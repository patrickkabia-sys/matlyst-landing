Air fryer frozen foods: time and temperature

Chicken nuggets, mozzarella sticks, tater tots, and fish sticks, from four sources. Where two sources list the same food, they sit side by side.

Cosori (2026) lists chicken nuggets at 385°F (196°C) for 7 to 12 minutes, and Mississippi State Extension (2024) lists 400°F (204°C) for 10 minutes. Cosori (2026) says most frozen foods go straight from the freezer to the basket. Treat the times as a place to start.

These are the settings from the appliance makers and the extension service. They are not package directions. Follow the package of the food you bought.

No thawing, one layer

Cosori (2026) says most frozen foods can go straight from the freezer to the basket with no thawing. It says ice crystals release moisture as they heat, and a full basket will steam the food instead of crisping it.

Cosori (2026) marks nuggets, tater tots, and most other frozen foods for shaking, and mozzarella sticks and fish sticks for flipping.

Frozen cheese is the exception

Illinois Extension (2024) says fresh cheese melts and becomes messy in an air fryer, and to use frozen and breaded cheese, such as frozen mozzarella sticks.

Packaged foods and the conversion rule

Philips (2013) gives a rule for pre-made packaged foods: take 70°F off the conventional oven temperature and cut the time by 50 percent. See the oven to air fryer page for how that compares with the other rules.

Questions about air fryer frozen foods

Do I thaw frozen food before the air fryer?

Cosori (2026) says most frozen foods can go straight from the freezer to the basket with no thawing.

How long do chicken nuggets take?

Cosori (2026) says 7 to 12 minutes at 385°F (196°C), Mississippi State Extension (2024) says 10 minutes at 400°F (204°C), and Philips (2013) says 6 to 10 minutes at 390°F (199°C).

How long do tater tots take?

Cosori (2026) says 16 to 24 minutes at 400°F (204°C). We found no second source for tater tots.
