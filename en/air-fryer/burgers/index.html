Air fryer burgers: time and temperature

The three sources give burgers three very different settings, from very hot to fairly low. Here they are side by side, with the USDA minimum for ground meat.

Cosori (2026) lists a four-ounce burger at 450°F (232°C) for 6 to 14 minutes. Mississippi State Extension (2024) lists 370°F (188°C) for 16 to 20 minutes, and Philips (2013) lists 360°F (182°C) for 7 to 14 minutes. Treat the times as a place to start. USDA FSIS (2025) sets the safe minimum for ground meats at 160°F (71°C).

A wide spread

Cosori (2026) uses the highest temperature of the three and a time that runs from short to long. Mississippi State Extension (2024) lists 16 to 20 minutes at a lower temperature. Cosori (2026) marks burgers for flipping.

The sources do not explain the gap. Cosori (2026) says thickness has the most direct effect on the time.

Internal temperature: the thermometer decides, not the clock

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 160°F (71°C) as the minimum internal temperature for ground meats, and 165°F (74°C) for ground poultry, so a turkey or chicken burger has a different number.

The times above only tell you when to start checking. The temperature decides, not the clock.

Questions about air fryer burgers

How long do burgers take in the air fryer?

Cosori (2026) says 6 to 14 minutes at 450°F (232°C), and Mississippi State Extension (2024) says 16 to 20 minutes at 370°F (188°C).

Do I flip burgers?

Cosori (2026) marks burgers for flipping.

What temperature are burgers done at?

USDA FSIS (2025) says 160°F (71°C) for ground meats and 165°F (74°C) for ground poultry.
