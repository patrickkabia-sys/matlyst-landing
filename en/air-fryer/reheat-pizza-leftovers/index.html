Reheat pizza and leftovers in an air fryer: time and temperature

Cosori has reheating settings for pizza, fries, fried chicken, steak, rice, and pasta. Illinois Extension and the USDA say what temperature the food should reach.

Cosori (2024) reheats thin-crust pizza at 350°F (177°C) for 3 to 4 minutes, and thick crust at 320°F (160°C) for 3 to 5 minutes. Illinois Extension (2024) says to set 350°F (177°C) and heat until the food reaches 165°F (74°C). Treat the times as a place to start.

Pizza

Cosori (2024) gives three settings, by the kind of slice.

SourceSliceTemperatureTime

Cosori (2026) says foods that need their crunch back, such as fries, wings, and breaded items, respond best to a higher temperature for a shorter time. Cosori (2024) adds a splash of water or sauce for pasta, to keep it from drying out.

Leftovers need to reach a safe temperature

Illinois Extension (2024) says to set the air fryer to 350°F (177°C) and cook until the food reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) for leftovers.

Questions about reheating

How long do you reheat pizza in an air fryer?

Cosori (2024) says 3 to 4 minutes at 350°F (177°C) for thin crust, and 3 to 5 minutes at 320°F (160°C) for thick crust.

What temperature should reheated food reach?

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) for leftovers, and Illinois Extension (2024) says 165°F (74°C) in the air fryer.

Is reheating in the air fryer better than the microwave?

Cosori (2024) says microwaving can make a pizza crust chewy, and that an air fryer can restore the crisp.
