Rules for converting a recipe

KitchenAid (2026) says to take 25°F off the oven temperature and cut the time by 20 percent. Illinois Extension (2024) says the same, with a drop of up to 30°F. Philips (2013) gives 70°F lower and half the time for pre-made packaged foods.

See all three, with examples, on the oven to air fryer page.

Warming up the air fryer

Cosori (2026) says a preheat of 3 minutes is enough for most foods, and Illinois Extension (2024) says typically 3 minutes. Philips (2013) says 3 minutes, and Instant Pot (2025) says 5 minutes or less. Cosori (2026) makes one exception: hard-boiled eggs go into a cold basket.

Safe internal temperatures

For poultry, meat, and fish the thermometer decides whether the food is done, not the clock. The times on our pages only say when to start checking. These are the minimums from USDA FSIS (2025), measured with a food thermometer:

FoodSourceMinimum
Poultry: breasts, legs, thighs, wingsUSDA FSIS (2025)165°F (74°C)
Ground meatsUSDA FSIS (2025)160°F (71°C)
Ground poultryUSDA FSIS (2025)165°F (74°C)
Beef, pork, veal, and lamb steaks, chops, and roastsUSDA FSIS (2025)145°F (63°C), then rest at least 3 minutes
Fish and shellfishUSDA FSIS (2025)145°F (63°C)
LeftoversUSDA FSIS (2025)165°F (74°C)

The USDA chart does not list bacon, hot dogs, vegetables, or potatoes, so we give no safe minimum for them.

Reading the times

Cosori (2026) says cooking times may vary by food thickness, starting temperature, and basket load. Mississippi State Extension (2024) says you may need to adjust times and temperatures for the size of the food and for your air fryer.

Cosori (2026) says a full basket reduces airflow and makes the time longer, so food should go in a single, even layer.

Questions about air fryer times

How long does chicken take in the air fryer?

It depends on the cut. Cosori (2026) lists boneless chicken breast at 390°F (199°C) for 15 to 22 minutes, and Cosori (2026) lists wings at 400°F (204°C) for 16 to 22 minutes. The thermometer decides: 165°F (74°C) for poultry, says USDA FSIS (2025).

Do I need to preheat the air fryer?

Cosori (2026) says a preheat of 3 minutes is enough for most foods. Philips (2013) says 3 minutes.

How do I convert an oven recipe to an air fryer?

KitchenAid (2026) says to take 25°F off the oven temperature and cut the time by 20 percent. The converter at the top of this page does that.

What temperature should meat reach?

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) for poultry, 160°F (71°C) for ground meats, and 145°F (63°C) for steaks and chops. Fish and shellfish are 145°F (63°C).
