Safe internal temperatures for meat, chicken, and fish

Cook chicken and turkey to 165°F, ground beef to 160°F, and fish to 145°F. Steaks, roasts, and pork chops need 145°F plus a 3-minute rest. Here are the USDA safe minimums and how to get a reading you can count on.

The safe minimums

chicken and turkey165°F
ground beef160°F
steaks and roasts145°F
pork chops145°F
fish145°F

Steaks, roasts, and chops: let them rest 3 minutes before you cut into them.
