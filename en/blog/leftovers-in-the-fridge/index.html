How long do leftovers last in the fridge?

Most leftovers last 3 to 4 days in the fridge. Here's how long the usual ones last, plus how to store and reheat them safely, based on USDA guidelines.

In the fridge

cooked chicken3 to 4 days
cooked fish3 to 4 days
soup and stew3 to 4 days
pizza3 to 4 days
hard-boiled eggs1 week

Based on USDA guidelines. If it smells or looks off, throw it out.

Keep them safe

fridge40°F or colder
into the fridge within2 hours
hot day, over 90°F1 hour
reheat to165°F
won't eat it in time?freeze it
