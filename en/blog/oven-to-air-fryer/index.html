How to convert an oven recipe to an air fryer

Air frying a recipe written for the oven? Turn the heat down 25 to 30°F and cut the time by 20%, then shake or flip halfway through. Here's the rule of thumb with a few examples, based on University of Illinois Extension guidelines.

The rule of thumb

oven temperature25 to 30°F lower
cooking time20% shorter
preheatabout 3 minutes
halfway throughshake or flip
the basketdon't overfill

Based on University of Illinois Extension. Check your air fryer's manual first.

A few examples

400°F for 20 min375°F, 16 min
425°F for 30 min400°F, 24 min
375°F for 15 min350°F, 12 min
350°F for 45 min325°F, 36 min

Cooking chicken? It's done at 165°F inside. A thermometer is the only way to be sure.
