How to tell if eggs are fresh: the float test

Not sure how old those eggs are? Put one in a bowl of water. If it sinks and lies flat, it's fresh. If it stands on end, it's getting old. If it floats, it's old, but it might still be fine: crack it into a bowl and give it a sniff and a look. Here's the whole test, plus how to store eggs so they last.

Sink or float?

sinks and lies flatfresh
sinks, stands on endolder
floatsold
smells off when crackedtoss it

An egg that floats can still be fine. Crack it into a bowl and give it a sniff and a look.

Store them right

fridge40°F or colder
store in the cartonnot the door
raw, in the shell3 to 5 weeks
hard-boiled1 week
out of the fridge2 hours, tops

Store-bought US eggs are washed, which strips their natural coating. Keep them cold.
