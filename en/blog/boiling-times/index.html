Boiling times for eggs and vegetables

Broccoli florets need 3 to 6 minutes in boiling water. Large eggs need 12 minutes in the hot water after you turn off the heat. Here are the times for everyday vegetables and hard-boiled eggs, from University of Maine and Oregon State University Extension.

Vegetables in the pot

broccoli florets3 to 6 min
green beansup to 10 min
corn on the cobabout 4 min
fresh shelled peas5 to 10 min
medium potatoes, wholeabout 30 to 40 min

A gentle boil cooks as fast as a rolling one. The water is just as hot.

Hard-boiled eggs, off the heat

cold water over eggsat least 1 inch
bring to a full boillid on, heat off
medium eggs9 min
large eggs12 min
extra-large eggs15 min

Then cool them in ice water. In the fridge: 2 days if peeled, up to a week in the shell.
