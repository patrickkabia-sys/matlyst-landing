How to double or halve a recipe

Doubling a recipe? Salt, spices, oil for the pan, and cooking time don't just double. Here's how much to use, plus how to cut a recipe in half, based on university extension guidelines.

When you double it

salt and spices1½x, then taste
oil or butter to sauténo need to double
check for doneness atthe original time
baking pan2 of the original
don't scale past4x the recipe

Yeast breads, cakes, pies, and custards are the exception. They don't scale well.

Cutting a recipe in half

¾ cup6 tbsp
⅓ cup2 tbsp + 2 tsp
¼ cup2 tbsp
1 tbsp1½ tsp
1 large egg2 tbsp, beaten

Refrigerate the leftover beaten egg and use it within 2 to 4 days.
