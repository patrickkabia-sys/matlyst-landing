Easy dinners in under 30 minutes

When the evening is packed with practice, homework, and late meetings, you need dinners that almost cook themselves. Here are quick meals with few ingredients and easy cleanup.

Ready in 15 minutes

  • Pesto pasta with cherry tomatoes. The pasta cooks, and everything else goes in the same pot.
  • Ham and cheese omelet. Serve with toast and a side salad.
  • Black bean quesadillas. Cook them in a skillet until the cheese melts.
  • Ramen with a soft egg. Broth, noodles, an egg, and green onions.

Ready in under 30 minutes

  • Sheet pan salmon with potatoes and broccoli. Everything on one pan.
  • Chicken stir-fry with rice. Chop everything first and the cooking goes fast.
  • Tacos. Ground beef or beans, and everyone builds their own.
  • Turkey burgers. On the stovetop in ten minutes.
  • Spaghetti carbonara. Eggs, bacon, parmesan, and black pepper.
  • Chickpea curry with rice. Canned chickpeas make it quick.

One pot, fewer dishes

  • Chicken and coconut curry. Serve with rice or naan.
  • Tomato and pasta soup. Warm, simple, and budget-friendly.
  • One-pot pasta. Pasta, tomatoes, garlic, and spinach cooked together.
  • Chili. Make it on Monday, eat it again on Wednesday.

How to make weeknight dinners easier

Quick dinners are mostly about prep. Three habits make weeknights easier.

  1. Keep go-to recipes. Pick ten dinners your family likes and save them in Matlyst. No more searching when you're already hungry.
  2. Plan before you shop. Put dinners in your meal plan and send the ingredients to your grocery list, so you have what you need.
  3. Use cook mode. One step at a time, with the ingredients for that step on screen and a timer for any step with a cooking time.

Easy dinner questions

What's a quick and easy dinner?

A dish with a few ingredients that's ready in under 30 minutes, like pesto pasta, an omelet, chicken stir-fry, or sheet pan salmon.

What can I make for dinner when I'm short on time?

Go for something that takes 15 minutes: quesadillas, ramen with an egg, or pesto pasta with cherry tomatoes.

How do I make weeknight dinners easier?

Keep go-to recipes, plan a few days at a time, and shop once. Matlyst keeps your recipes, meal plan, and grocery list in one place.
