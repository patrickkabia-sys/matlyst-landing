Who is behind Matlyst

Matlyst is made by Patrick Kabia. He is a refrigeration technician by trade.

Matlyst is run by a private individual in Norway, and Patrick is the data controller for the personal data in the app. What we store and why is in the privacy policy, and the terms are posted too.

Why Matlyst was made

Patrick never trained as a chef. He trained as a refrigeration technician. Food is his love language, and he wanted to stop losing the recipes he cooks from.

As an apprentice, his job took him into some of the best restaurant kitchens in his hometown. He was never the one cooking. He was the one fixing the walk-in cooler before the first order came in. From there he got a close look at how professionals work: nothing is random, and everything has a place, a label, and a date.

At home, his recipes lived in eight places: notes, OneNote, a recipe app, bookmarks, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and favorites. He had the cooking, but no system. So he built the system he had seen in those kitchens, for a normal family dinner.

Save and find your recipes

Matlyst is a recipe app for iPhone and Android. You save recipes from websites, Instagram, and TikTok, or from a photo, and get them as a tidy list of ingredients and steps. You can search your recipes and scale them. Matlyst is free to start.

What the trade means for the content

Refrigeration is Patrick’s trade: temperature, time, and what happens to food in the fridge and the freezer. Matlyst has pages on shelf life, chilling, and freezing, and there he knows the subject.

That does not mean the numbers on the pages are his own judgment. The numbers come from named sources, for example USDA FSIS, and each page shows which ones. The sources are what make the numbers trustworthy, not the trade.

How the content is made and checked

The text on this site is written with the help of AI and reviewed by Patrick, and the numbers have sources.

These are the rules we follow when a page has numbers about food safety, shelf life, chilling, or thawing.

  • A source for every number. Every number has a source with an address and the date we opened and read it.
  • Sources are read, not guessed. We do not use numbers from memory, or from other sites that only repeat numbers.
  • Rules of thumb are labeled. Amounts per person and cooking times are guidelines, and the pages say so.
  • No source, no number. If we cannot find a source, the number comes out of the page. We do not make up our own numbers.
  • No health claims. We do not give diet or health advice.
  • No recipes of our own. Matlyst gives answers and tools. The recipes are yours.

The page on how long food lasts is an example. Each number sits in a table with its source, its address, and the date we accessed it.

Matlyst is not affiliated with the sources we point to.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, or a source has changed, we want to know. Write to hei@matlyst-app.no and tell us the page and the number. We check against the source, fix the page, and update the date.

Questions about Matlyst

Who makes Matlyst?

Matlyst is made by Patrick Kabia, a refrigeration technician by trade. Matlyst is run by a private individual in Norway.

How do I get in touch?

Write to hei@matlyst-app.no. We answer questions about the app, privacy, and the content on our pages.

Where do the numbers on the pages come from?

From named sources such as USDA FSIS. Every number has a source with an address and the date we read it, and numbers we cannot find a source for are taken out.

What should I do if I find a mistake?

Write to hei@matlyst-app.no and tell us the page and the number. We check against the source, fix the page, and update the date.

Does Matlyst give health advice or its own recipes?

No. Matlyst gives answers and tools, not health advice and not recipes of its own. You save the recipes you use yourself.

What happens to my information?

It is in the privacy policy. It also says that Matlyst never sells your information.
