Save recipes from Alletiders Kogebog

How to save a recipe from Alletiders Kogebog in Matlyst while keeping the source.

How to save the recipe

  1. Open a recipe on Alletiders Kogebog.
  2. Open the share menu and choose Matlyst, or paste the link into recipe import.
  3. Review the ingredients, quantities and instructions before saving.

Source and ownership

Matlyst keeps the source link when it is available. Matlyst is not affiliated with Alletiders Kogebog and does not claim ownership of its text or images.
