Good to know

Suggestions are a guide. Check quantities, storage dates and whether every ingredient is still safe to use.

How the pages are made

  • No recipes. Each page has kinds of dishes, why they work, and what else you need, in rough terms.
  • Storage only with a source. Numbers about storage come from FoodSafety.gov and USDA FSIS, with links at the bottom. Where we don’t have a source, the page says so and gives no number.
  • The numbers are a guide. They come from the sources and are not a guarantee for a single item.

Questions and answers

Does ingredient search create new recipes?

No. It matches your own collection. Ask Matlyst can create or adapt a dish.

Are the pages free?

Yes, you don’t need the app to use them. Searching your own recipes and the pantry are free too. Dinner ideas from the pantry are a Pro feature.

Do the pages have recipes?

No. They list kinds of dishes that fit, why, and what else you need.

Where do the numbers come from?

From FoodSafety.gov and USDA FSIS. The sources are listed at the bottom of each page.
