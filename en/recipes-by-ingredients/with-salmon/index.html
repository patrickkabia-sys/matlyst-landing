What to make with salmon

Salmon works for a weeknight dinner or a lunch the next day. Here’s what to make, and what to do with the rest.

Salmon fits salmon cakes, salmon salad, pasta, rice bowls, and chowder. USDA FSIS (2020) says cooked leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator, and FoodSafety.gov (2023) gives 2 to 3 months in the freezer for fatty fish such as salmon.

Five dishes that fit

Salmon cakes

Why it works
Flaked cooked salmon mixes with egg and breadcrumbs and pan-fries into patties. A classic way to stretch a small fillet.
You also need
Egg, breadcrumbs, onion, and lemon.

Salmon salad or sandwiches

Why it works
Flake cold salmon and mix with something creamy for a quick lunch.
You also need
Mayonnaise or yogurt, celery, and bread or greens.

Creamy salmon pasta

Why it works
Cooked salmon warms in a cream sauce with pasta.
You also need
Pasta, cream or cream cheese, and spinach or peas.

Salmon rice bowls

Why it works
Flaked salmon over rice with vegetables and a drizzle of sauce.
You also need
Rice, cucumber or avocado, and soy sauce.

Salmon chowder

Why it works
Salmon adds flavor to a potato and corn soup. A little goes far.
You also need
Potatoes, corn, onion, and milk or cream.

How to store the rest

Questions and answers

How long does cooked salmon keep in the fridge?

USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers keep 3 to 4 days.

Can I freeze salmon?

Yes. FoodSafety.gov (2023) gives 2 to 3 months in the freezer for fatty fish.

What’s a good way to use a small piece of salmon?

Salmon cakes or a salad. Both stretch a small fillet.
