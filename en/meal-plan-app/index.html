Meal planner

A weekly meal planner built on your own recipes

Pick from the recipes you've already saved, put them on the days that work, and the grocery list follows. Every dinner on it is something your household actually eats.

How to plan your week

You decide what goes on the plan. Matlyst doesn't fill the week for you.

  1. Pick a day. Open Meal Plan and tap the day you want to plan.
  2. Add a recipe. Choose from the recipes you've saved. Plan a single dinner or the whole week.
  3. Send it to the grocery list. Add the ingredients for one recipe or a whole day to your grocery list.

No idea for Thursday? The Home screen suggests dinners from your own recipes and what you have in your pantry.

The meal plan and the grocery list are free. Creating or joining a household to share them takes Matlyst Pro.

Meal planning without the spreadsheet

A plan only helps if it's quick to make. Put a recipe on Monday, another on Wednesday, and leave the weekend open. It takes a couple of minutes, not a Sunday afternoon.

One grocery list for the whole week

Everything you plan lands on one list. Matching items are combined into one line, and the list is grouped by aisle, so you're not walking back across the store for the onions.

Read more about the grocery list.

Plan together

With Matlyst Pro, your household shares the meal plan and the grocery list. One person plans, another shops, and everyone sees the same week.

Cook it when the day comes

Open the recipe in cook mode and go one step at a time, with the ingredients for that step right on the screen. New to Matlyst? Start by building your recipe collection.

Meal planner questions

Is there a free meal planner app with a grocery list?

Yes. Matlyst's meal plan and grocery list are free. You plan with recipes you've saved yourself, from TikTok, Instagram, websites, or your own recipe cards.

Can a meal planner make my grocery list?

Yes. Add a recipe or a whole day from your meal plan to the grocery list, and Matlyst combines matching ingredients into one line.

Does Matlyst make meal plans for me?

No. You choose the recipes. When you need ideas, the Home screen suggests dinners from your saved recipes.

Can I share my meal plan with my partner?

Yes, with Matlyst Pro. Invite your household, and you share the meal plan and the grocery list.

Is there a weekly meal planner for iPhone and Android?

Matlyst works on both. Get it on the App Store or Google Play.
