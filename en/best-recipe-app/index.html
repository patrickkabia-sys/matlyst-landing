Best recipe apps: eight recipe organizers compared

We make Matlyst, so we have a stake in this. That’s why every fact here has a source and a date. Here are eight recipe apps you can get in the US, compared on what the apps say about themselves.

No app is best for everyone. If you save from Instagram and TikTok, Matlyst, Pestle, ReciMe, and Recipe Keeper all say they do it, and Paprika does it with Premium. ReciMe’s free plan allows five imports a week. If you want one app on iPhone, Mac, Android, and Windows, look at Paprika or Recipe Keeper. If you only use Apple devices, Crouton and Mela are built for them, and Mela is a one-time purchase. If you want a big library of recipes to browse, Samsung Food has one.

Who wrote this page

We make Matlyst, so we have a stake in this. That’s why every fact here has a source and a date. Matlyst is one of the apps on this page. Everything we say about the other apps comes from their own App Store and Google Play listings and their own websites, and each card links there. Last checked November 21, 2026.

The short answer: pick by what you need

  • You save from Instagram and TikTok: Matlyst, Pestle, ReciMe, and Recipe Keeper say they import from both. Paprika does it with Premium.
  • You want to use it for free: ReciMe allows five imports a week, Matlyst five a month, Paprika up to 50 recipes on one device, and Pestle 15 recipes.
  • You don’t want a subscription: Mela unlocks everything with a one-time purchase. Recipe Keeper sells a Pro upgrade in the app, and Pestle has a lifetime option.
  • You use a PC or Windows: Paprika and Recipe Keeper both run on Windows.
  • You only use Apple devices: Crouton runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision, and Apple Watch. Mela runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
  • You cook from US recipes and metric ones: Crouton, Paprika, Pestle, ReciMe, and Recipe Keeper say they convert units. Matlyst is metric by default, and with Units set to US it keeps the cups and °F a recipe uses.
  • You want new recipes, not just your own: Samsung Food has a large library of public recipes.

How to save a recipe and plan the week in Matlyst

If you pick Matlyst, share a link or a post from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or a website to Matlyst, or paste the link in the app. Check the draft before you save it, since AI reads the recipe and can get an amount wrong. Then put the recipe on a day in your meal plan, and the ingredients go to your grocery list.

How we chose

We included apps that are in the US App Store and have saving and organizing recipes as one of their main features. Most are on Google Play too. We used our own judgment to pick them, and an app that isn’t here isn’t necessarily worse.

Every fact comes from the app’s own listing in the US App Store, from Google Play in the US, and from the app’s own website. We checked them on November 21, 2026. Each card links to its sources.

  • Alphabetical order. The apps are listed by name, and the order isn’t a ranking.
  • “Not stated” means we didn’t find it in the sources. It doesn’t mean the app can’t do it.
  • No stars or ratings. They change from week to week, and they say little about what suits you.
  • No prices in dollars. Prices change and can differ between the App Store and Google Play. You’ll see the price in the store before you buy.
  • No logos or screenshots from other apps. The names belong to their owners, and Matlyst isn’t affiliated with any of them.

Questions and answers

What is the best recipe app?

It depends on what you need. If you save from Instagram and TikTok, look at Matlyst, Pestle, ReciMe, or Recipe Keeper. For free imports from social media every week, look at ReciMe. On a PC or Windows, look at Paprika or Recipe Keeper. If you’d rather pay once, look at Mela.

Is there a free recipe organizer app?

Yes. Every app on this page is free to download. The free versions have different limits, such as five imports a week in ReciMe, five a month in Matlyst, 50 recipes on one device in Paprika, and 15 recipes in Pestle.

Which recipe apps don’t need a subscription?

Mela unlocks all features with a one-time purchase. Recipe Keeper sells a Pro upgrade in the app, and Pestle Pro has a lifetime option. The others say they use a subscription or don’t state it.

Which recipe apps handle both cups and metric?

Crouton, Paprika, Pestle, ReciMe, and Recipe Keeper say they convert units or measurements. Matlyst is metric by default, and with Units set to US it keeps the cups, ounces, and °F the recipe used.

Why are there no stars or prices?

Ratings and prices change often. We only write what we can point to in the app’s own listing or website, with a date.

Can this page be neutral if Matlyst made it?

We have a stake in this, and we say so at the top. That’s why every fact links to its source and has a date, so you can check it yourself.
