Cooking times

How long does it take to cook a turkey?

FSIS gives turkey roasting times by weight band, at 325°F (163°C). A 16-pound turkey, unstuffed, takes 3 3/4 to 4 1/4 hours. Enter your turkey's weight below. The turkey is done when a thermometer reads 165°F (74°C), not when the timer rings.

Roasting time calculator

The calculator looks up your weight in the USDA FSIS (2024) table below. FSIS gives a range for each band of weights, so you get a range back, not an exact time. “These times are approximate and should always be used in conjunction with a properly placed food thermometer.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

Before it goes in the oven

USDA FSIS (2024) says to set the oven temperature no lower than 325°F (163°C). “Preheating is not necessary.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)

“Be sure the turkey is completely thawed.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)

The turkey goes breast-side up on a flat wire rack in a shallow roasting pan, 2 to 2 1/2 inches deep. A tent of aluminum foil may go loosely over the breast for the first 1 to 1 1/2 hours, then come off for browning.

Why your turkey may cook faster or slower

USDA FSIS (2019) lists some of the reasons:

  • “A stuffed turkey takes longer to cook.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)
  • “Dark roasting pans cook faster than shiny metals.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)
  • “An oven cooking bag can accelerate cooking time.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)
  • “Temperature of the oven may be inaccurate.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)

How to know it's done

A whole turkey is safe at 165°F (74°C) throughout. “Check the temperature in the innermost part of the thigh and wing and the thickest part of the breast.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)

A frozen turkey

“It is safe to roast a turkey from the frozen state; however, it will take longer than a fresh or thawed bird.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

USDA FSIS (2026) gives this rule for roasting from frozen: use the timing for the size of turkey you have, then add 50 percent of that time. “Roasting time is approximate, so check the turkey often toward the end of the estimated cooking time.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

Questions about turkey

How long does a 16-pound turkey take to roast?

USDA FSIS (2024) gives 3 3/4 to 4 1/4 hours unstuffed, and 4 to 4 1/4 hours stuffed, for a whole turkey of 14 to 18 pounds, at 325°F (163°C).

How long does a 10-pound turkey take?

A turkey of 8 to 12 pounds takes 2 3/4 to 3 hours unstuffed and 3 to 3 1/2 hours stuffed, says USDA FSIS (2024).

How long should the turkey rest?

USDA FSIS (2019) says to let the bird stand 20 minutes before carving.

Is it safe to roast a turkey from frozen?

USDA FSIS (2026) says it is safe to roast a turkey from the frozen state, but it will take longer. Add 50 percent to the time.

Should the turkey be stuffed?

“For optimum safety, cook stuffing in a casserole.” (USDA FSIS, 2019)
