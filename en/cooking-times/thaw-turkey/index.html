Cooking times

How long does it take to thaw a turkey?

In the refrigerator, FSIS says to allow about 24 hours for each 4 to 5 pounds. In cold water it is about 30 minutes per pound. Pick the method and type in the weight below.

Thawing time calculator

The calculator looks your weight up in the USDA FSIS (2023) tables below. A weight on a line between two rows is in both.

3 to 4

14 pounds, refrigerator: 3 to 4 days

Refrigerator thawing

USDA FSIS (2023) says to allow approximately 24 hours for each 4 to 5 pounds in a refrigerator set at 40°F (4°C) or below.

Whole turkey weightTime in the refrigerator
4 to 12 pounds1 to 3 days
12 to 16 pounds3 to 4 days
16 to 20 pounds4 to 5 days
20 to 24 pounds5 to 6 days

Source: USDA FSIS (2023), “Turkey Basics: Safe Thawing”. Read November 21, 2026.

A thawed turkey can remain in the refrigerator for 1 to 2 days before cooking. “Foods thawed in the refrigerator can be refrozen without cooking but there may be some loss of quality.” (USDA FSIS, 2023)

Cold water thawing

USDA FSIS (2023) says to allow about 30 minutes per pound. “First be sure the turkey is in a leak-proof plastic bag to prevent cross-contamination and to prevent the turkey from absorbing water, resulting in a watery product.” (USDA FSIS, 2023) The water changes every 30 minutes.

Whole turkey weightTime in cold water
4 to 12 pounds2 to 6 hours
12 to 16 pounds6 to 8 hours
16 to 20 pounds8 to 10 hours
20 to 24 pounds10 to 12 hours

“After cooking, meat from the turkey can be refrozen.” (USDA FSIS, 2023)

Microwave thawing

USDA FSIS (2023) says to follow the microwave maker's instructions. “A turkey thawed in the microwave must be cooked immediately.” (USDA FSIS, 2023)

What not to do

“Thawing a frozen turkey on the kitchen counter or in hot water is not safe.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

USDA FSIS (2023) adds that a package of frozen meat or poultry left thawing on the counter more than 2 hours is not at a safe temperature.

Three safe ways, and the first step

“There are three safe ways to thaw food: in the refrigerator, in cold water, and in the microwave oven.” (USDA FSIS, 2023)

Questions about thawing a turkey

How many days does it take to thaw a 14 pound turkey?

A weight of 12 to 16 pounds takes 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator, says USDA FSIS (2023).

How long does cold water thawing take?

About 30 minutes per pound, with the water changed every 30 minutes.

How long can a thawed turkey wait in the fridge?

1 to 2 days before cooking, according to USDA FSIS (2023).

Can I thaw a turkey on the counter?

“Thawing a frozen turkey on the kitchen counter or in hot water is not safe.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)
