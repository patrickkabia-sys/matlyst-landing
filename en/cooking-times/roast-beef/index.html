Cooking times

How long does it take to cook roast beef?

A bone-in rib roast of 4 to 6 pounds takes 23 to 25 minutes per pound at 325°F (163°C), says USDA FSIS (2024). A round or rump roast takes 30 to 35 minutes per pound. Roast beef is safe at 145°F (63°C).

Times from the beef page

RoastWeightTime
Rib roast, bone in, at 325°F4 to 6 pounds23 to 25 minutes per pound
Rib roast, boneless, rolled4 to 6 poundsadd 5 to 8 minutes per pound to the times above
Round or rump roast, at 325°F2 1/2 to 4 pounds30 to 35 minutes per pound
Tenderloin, whole, at 425°F (218°C)4 to 6 pounds45 to 60 minutes total

Source: USDA FSIS (2024), “Beef from Farm to Table”. Accessed November 21, 2026.

“Times are based on beef at refrigerator temperature” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

“Remember that appliances and outdoor grills can vary in heat.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

The two FSIS pages do not give the same time for every cut. The bone-in rib roast is 23 to 25 minutes per pound on one and 23 to 30 minutes per pound on the other. Both are at 325°F (163°C). Use the thermometer to settle it.

Why boneless takes longer

“Boned and rolled meats require more cooking time per pound than bone-in cuts because it takes longer for the heat to penetrate through the solid meat.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

Roasting basics

“To roast, meat is placed on a rack in a shallow, uncovered pan and is cooked by the indirect dry heat of an oven.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

When is it done?

USDA FSIS (2024) says to cook raw beef steaks and roasts to a minimum internal temperature of 145°F (63°C), and to rest the meat at least 3 minutes before carving. See roast beef internal temperature.

Questions about roast beef

How long do you cook a 5-pound rib roast?

A bone-in rib roast of 4 to 6 pounds takes 23 to 25 minutes per pound, per USDA FSIS (2024). A 5-pound roast falls inside that weight range.

How long does a beef tenderloin take?

A whole tenderloin of 4 to 6 pounds takes 45 to 60 minutes total at 425°F (218°C), according to USDA FSIS (2024).

How long does a round roast take?

30 to 35 minutes per pound for a roast of 2 1/2 to 4 pounds at 325°F (163°C).
