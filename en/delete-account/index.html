Account and privacy

Delete your Matlyst account

Delete your Matlyst account and personal data in the app, or contact us if you no longer have access.

Delete the account in the app

  1. Open Profile in Matlyst.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Your recipes, folders, meal plan, shopping list, Pantry data and profile are scheduled for deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Email hei@matlyst-app.no from the address connected to your account. We may ask for information needed to verify that the account belongs to you.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained when required by law, for security, or to document purchases and legal claims. Read the privacy policy for details.
