Recipe organizer

The recipe organizer app for recipes you actually cook

Screenshots, bookmarks, saved reels, your mom's recipe card. Matlyst puts them in one digital recipe book you can search, sort into folders, and cook from.

How to organize your recipes in Matlyst

Start with the recipes you make most. You don't have to move everything on day one.

  1. Save it from anywhere. Share a TikTok, an Instagram post, or a web page to Matlyst from the Share Sheet. Or open Add recipe and paste a link or type it in. With Matlyst Pro, you can also take a photo of a recipe card or cookbook page.
  2. Check the recipe. Matlyst splits it into an ingredient list and numbered steps. Fix anything that looks off, then save it.
  3. Pick a folder. Weeknights, Holidays, whatever works for you. Mark the ones you love as favorites.

Moving from Paprika? Import your Paprika export and your categories show up as folders.

The free plan includes a set number of imports each month, and the app shows how many you've used. Matlyst Pro adds unlimited imports and photo import.

Why an app beats saved posts and screenshots

A saved reel is a video in a feed. A screenshot is a picture you zoom into with flour on your hands. In Matlyst, every recipe has the same layout: an ingredient list, steps in order, and a link back to where it came from. That makes your recipes easy to search and easy to cook from.

Find any recipe in seconds

Search by title or ingredient, open a folder, or go straight to your favorites. Got chicken thighs and half a bag of spinach? Search for them and see what you've already saved.

From recipe book to dinner

Organizing is only half the job. Add a recipe to your meal plan, send the ingredients to your grocery list, and cook it in cook mode, one step per screen with the ingredients for that step right there.

Your recipes stay yours

Your collection is private. Matlyst keeps the source link so the creator gets credit, and you can edit any recipe to make it your own.

Recipe organizer questions

What's the best way to organize recipes?

Keep them in one place, in the same format, with a way to search them. A recipe organizer app does that for you. Matlyst turns links, posts, and photos into recipes with an ingredient list and steps, and you sort them into folders.

Is there a free recipe organizer app?

Yes. Matlyst is free to download, and saving recipes, folders, search, the meal plan, and the grocery list all work without paying. Matlyst Pro adds unlimited imports, photo import, and household sharing.

Can I save recipes from TikTok and Instagram?

Yes. Share a public post to Matlyst and it reads the recipe from the caption. It doesn't transcribe what's said in the video, so if the recipe is only spoken, type in the parts you need.

Can I add my own family recipes?

Yes. Type them in, or take a photo of a recipe card or a cookbook page with Matlyst Pro.

Does Matlyst work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Matlyst is on the App Store and Google Play.

Can I bring my recipes over from Paprika?

Yes. Export your recipes from Paprika and open the file in Matlyst. The Paprika import guide on this site walks you through it.
