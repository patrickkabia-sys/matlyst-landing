Internal temperature

What internal temperature are egg dishes done?

Cook egg dishes, such as a frittata or a quiche, to 160°F (71°C). For eggs cooked on their own, FoodSafety.gov (2024) says to cook until the yolk and the white are firm.

Egg dishes

FoodSafety.gov (2024) lists 160°F (71°C) for egg dishes, such as frittata and quiche.

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 160°F (71°C) for eggs.

USDA FSIS (2024) says to cook casseroles and other dishes containing eggs to a safe minimum internal temperature of 160°F (71°C).

Eggs on their own

“Cook until yolk and white are firm” (FoodSafety.gov, 2024)

Dishes with raw egg

USDA FSIS (2024) says: cook dishes containing raw eggs immediately, or refrigerate and cook within 24 hours.

Meringue pies

USDA FSIS (2024) says meringue-topped pies should be safe if baked at 350°F (177°C) for about 15 minutes.

Hard-cooked eggs

“A green ring on a hard-cooked yolk can be a result of overcooking as the sulfur and iron compounds in the egg react on the yolk's surface.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

Questions about egg dishes

What temperature is quiche done?

160°F (71°C), says FoodSafety.gov (2024), which lists quiche and frittata as examples of egg dishes.

How do I know a fried egg is done?

“Cook until yolk and white are firm” (FoodSafety.gov, 2024)

How long can a raw egg dish wait before baking?

USDA FSIS (2024) says to cook it immediately, or refrigerate and cook within 24 hours.
