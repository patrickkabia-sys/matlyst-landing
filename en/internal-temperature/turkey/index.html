Internal temperature

What internal temperature should a turkey reach?

A turkey is done when the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C). FoodSafety.gov (2024) and USDA FSIS (2025) agree, and the FSIS Thanksgiving guide repeats it (USDA FSIS, 2026). Stuffing that cooks inside the bird has to reach the same number.

The number, from three federal pages

FoodSafety.gov (2024) lists 165°F (74°C) for a whole bird, breasts, legs, thighs, wings, ground poultry, and stuffing inside poultry.

USDA FSIS (2025) lists 165°F (74°C) for all poultry.

“Regardless of how it is cooked, the internal temperature of cooked turkey must be a minimum of 165 degrees F.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

Three spots to check

“For a whole turkey, measure in three areas: Thickest part of the breast Innermost part of the wing Innermost part of the thigh.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

“Take care to ensure the thermometer does not touch any bones while measuring the temperature, as this will give an inaccurate reading.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

Stuffing

USDA FSIS (2019) says the turkey and the center of the stuffing must both reach 165°F (74°C).

“USDA does not recommend stuffing a whole turkey because it increases the risk of cross-contamination and takes longer to cook.” (USDA FSIS, 2026)

Resting the turkey

USDA FSIS (2019) says to let the bird stand 20 minutes before removing the stuffing and carving it, so the juices can set.

A pink turkey

USDA FSIS (2024) says turkey can remain pink even after cooking to a safe minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

“The meat of smoked turkey is always pink.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

“Only by using a food thermometer can one accurately determine that poultry has reached a safe minimum internal temperature of 165 F throughout the product.” (USDA FSIS, 2024)

Questions about turkey

Where do I put the thermometer in a turkey?

USDA FSIS (2026) says to measure in three areas: the thickest part of the breast, the innermost part of the wing, and the innermost part of the thigh.

Is it safe to eat turkey that is still pink?

USDA FSIS (2024) says turkey can remain pink even after cooking to a safe minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Only a thermometer can tell you.

Does the stuffing need to reach 165°F too?

Yes. USDA FSIS (2019) says the center of the stuffing must reach 165°F (74°C).

How long should a turkey rest?

USDA FSIS (2019) says to let the bird stand 20 minutes before carving.
