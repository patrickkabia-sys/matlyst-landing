Why recipes use different units

Most of the world cooks by weight and in metric units. The United States cooks mostly by volume, in cups and spoons, and measures weight in ounces and pounds. Nordic recipes add the deciliter. A recipe found online can be written in any of them, which is why a good converter saves time.

Unit converter questions

How many ml are in a US cup?

A US cup is 236.6 ml. Many recipes round it to 240 ml, and a metric cup in some countries is 250 ml.

How many ml are in a US tablespoon?

A US tablespoon is 14.8 ml, usually rounded to 15 ml. In Australia a tablespoon is 20 ml.

How many grams are in an ounce?

An ounce of weight is 28.35 grams. Fluid ounces measure volume instead: 1 US fluid ounce is 29.57 ml.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9. For example, 350°F is about 177°C.

Does Matlyst convert recipe units?

With the Units setting on Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds from a recipe are converted to milliliters and grams on import. The setting applies to new imports.
