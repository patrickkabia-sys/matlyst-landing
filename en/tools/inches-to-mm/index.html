Inches to millimeters, with a chart, the formula, and what to watch for in a recipe.

1 in = 25.4 mm

Short answer: 1 in = 25.4 mm. To convert inches to millimeters, multiply by 25.4.

Where recipes use inches and millimeters

Inches are used in US baking pan sizes and dough thickness. Millimeters are used in dough thickness and fine cuts. That is why you convert whenever a recipe is written for one system and your tools use the other.

Measuring tips

Measure a pan across the top from inside edge to inside edge, not across the outside rim. A pan one size off changes baking time, so check the cake earlier if yours is larger than the recipe says.

A quick estimate: an inch is about two and a half centimeters, and a 9 inch pan is about 23 cm.

Common mistakes when converting

  • Measuring a pan from the outside rim instead of the inside.
  • Swapping a 9 inch pan for a 20 cm pan without checking, which is a different area.
  • Forgetting that a deeper pan needs a longer bake.
  • Reading mm as cm when rolling dough.
  • Not rounding to a pan size you can actually buy.

Converting a whole recipe

When a recipe is written in inches and you cook in millimeters, convert every amount in one pass and keep the ratios the same. Write the new amounts next to the old ones so you can check your work. If you scale the recipe up or down as well, convert first and scale second, so you do not round twice.

Doing this for one recipe is fine. Doing it for a hundred saved recipes is why people use an app.

In to mm questions

How many millimeters are in 1 inch?

1 inch is 25.4 millimeters.

How many inches are in 1 millimeter?

1 millimeter is 0.03937 inches.

How do I convert in to mm without a calculator?

Multiply by 25.4. For example, 2 in is 50.8 mm.

Is this conversion exact?

The factor is exact, but recipes usually round it, for example 236.6 ml for a US cup to 240 ml. Use the rounded number only when the recipe is forgiving.

What is in to mm used for in cooking?

Inches are used in US baking pan sizes and dough thickness. Millimeters are used in dough thickness and fine cuts.

Can I convert mm back to in?

Yes. Use the swap button in the calculator, or open the mm to in page. 1 millimeter is 0.03937 inches.

Does Matlyst convert units in recipes?

When you import a recipe, Matlyst shows the amounts in metric or keeps the source amounts, depending on the Units setting in Settings. With Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds are converted to milliliters and grams. The setting applies to new imports.
