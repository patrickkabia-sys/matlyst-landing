Pounds to kilograms (lb to kg)

Pounds to kilograms, with a chart, the formula, and what to watch for in a recipe.

0.4536

1 lb = 0.4536 kg

Short answer: 1 lb = 0.4536 kg. To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.4536 (or divide by 2.205).

How to convert pounds to kilograms

The rule: multiply by 0.4536 (or divide by 2.205). In a formula: kg = lb × 0.4536.

Where recipes use pounds and kilograms

A pound is 0.4536 kilograms. Two pounds is just over 900 g, which is why a 1 kg pack is a little more than 2 lb.

Measuring tips

A kitchen scale beats any measuring cup, especially for flour and sugar. Set the bowl on the scale, zero it, and add the ingredient. If you only have cups, remember that a cup of flour and a cup of sugar do not weigh the same.

A quick estimate: a pound is a little less than half a kilo, and a kilo is a little more than two pounds. An ounce is about 28 grams.

Common mistakes when converting

  • Treating ounces on the package as fluid ounces. Weight and volume are different measures.
  • Using a cup for flour and expecting the same grams every time. Scooping packs flour; spooning and leveling gives less.
  • Rounding a pound to 500 g when the recipe is sensitive, like bread. A pound is 454 g.
  • Forgetting to zero the scale after adding the bowl.
  • Converting one ingredient and not the others, so the ratios drift.

Converting a whole recipe

When a recipe is written in pounds and you cook in kilograms, convert every amount in one pass and keep the ratios the same. Write the new amounts next to the old ones so you can check your work. If you scale the recipe up or down as well, convert first and scale second, so you do not round twice.

Doing this for one recipe is fine. Doing it for a hundred saved recipes is why people use an app.

Lb to kg questions

How many kilograms are in 1 pound?

1 pound is 0.4536 kilograms.

How many pounds are in 1 kilogram?

1 kilogram is 2.205 pounds.

How do I convert lb to kg without a calculator?

Multiply by 0.4536 (or divide by 2.205). For example, 2 lb is 0.9072 kg.

Is this conversion exact?

The factor is exact, but recipes usually round it, for example 236.6 ml for a US cup to 240 ml. Use the rounded number only when the recipe is forgiving.

What is lb to kg used for in cooking?

Pounds are used in US recipes and grocery packaging for meat, produce, and flour. Kilograms are used in metric recipes for meat, potatoes, flour, and bulk buying.

Can I convert kg back to lb?

Yes. Use the swap button in the calculator, or open the kg to lb page. 1 kilogram is 2.205 pounds.

Does Matlyst convert units in recipes?

When you import a recipe, Matlyst shows the amounts in metric or keeps the source amounts, depending on the Units setting in Settings. With Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds are converted to milliliters and grams. The setting applies to new imports.
