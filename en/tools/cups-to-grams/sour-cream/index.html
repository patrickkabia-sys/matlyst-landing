Sour cream: cups to grams

1 cup of sour cream weighs 230 g (8.1 oz). Type an amount to convert.

Ingredient
Sour creamChange ingredient
460

2 cups sour cream = 460 g

Short answer: 1 cup of sour cream is 230 g. 1 tablespoon is 14 g and 1 teaspoon is 4.8 g, calculated from the cup weight.

Source: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy, “Cream, sour, cultured.”

Convert grams back to cups

Divide the weight in grams by 230 to get cups of sour cream. Going the other way, multiply cups by 230.

cups=grams÷230

Examples

  • 50 g0.22 cups
  • 100 g0.43 cups
  • 250 g1.1 cups
  • 500 g2.2 cups

1 pound of sour cream is 454 g, or 2 cups. 1 kilogram (1,000 g) is 4.3 cups.

Ounces and fluid ounces

Fluid ounces measure volume, and ounces measure weight (1 oz = 28.35 g). A cup holds 8 fl oz. 1 cup of sour cream weighs 8.1 oz.

USDA data

USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy, FDC ID 171257: “Cream, sour, cultured.” Portions are listed in USDA's own wording.

PortionGrams
cupUsed in the calculator230 g
tbsp12 g

USDA lists 1 tbsp of sour cream as 12 g. The calculator and the chart use the cup weight, which gives 14 g for 1 tbsp.

Questions about sour cream

How many grams are in a cup of sour cream?

1 cup of sour cream weighs 230 g (8.1 oz).

How many cups is 100 grams of sour cream?

100 g of sour cream is 0.43 cups. Divide the grams by 230.

How many grams are in a tablespoon of sour cream?

1 tablespoon weighs 14 g, and 1 teaspoon weighs 4.8 g. Both are calculated from the cup weight.

Does a cup of sour cream weigh the same as a cup of yogurt?

1 cup of sour cream weighs 230 g, and 1 cup of plain whole-milk yogurt weighs 245 g.
