Sugar: cups to grams

1 cup of granulated sugar weighs 200 g (7.1 oz). Type an amount to convert.

Ingredient
SugarChange ingredient
400

2 cups sugar = 400 g

Short answer: 1 cup of granulated sugar is 200 g. 1 tablespoon is 13 g and 1 teaspoon is 4.2 g, calculated from the cup weight.

Source: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy, “Sugars, granulated.”

Convert grams back to cups

Divide the weight in grams by 200 to get cups of sugar. Going the other way, multiply cups by 200.

cups=grams÷200

Examples

  • 50 g0.25 cups
  • 100 g0.5 cups
  • 250 g1.3 cups
  • 500 g2.5 cups

1 pound of sugar is 454 g, or 2.3 cups. 1 kilogram (1,000 g) is 5 cups.

Ounces and fluid ounces

Fluid ounces measure volume, and ounces measure weight (1 oz = 28.35 g). A cup holds 8 fl oz. 1 cup of sugar weighs 7.1 oz.

Questions about sugar

How many grams are in a cup of sugar?

1 cup of granulated sugar weighs 200 g (7.1 oz).

How many cups is 100 grams of sugar?

100 g of sugar is 0.5 cups. Divide the grams by 200.

How many grams are in a tablespoon of sugar?

1 tablespoon weighs 13 g, and 1 teaspoon weighs 4.2 g. Both are calculated from the cup weight.

Does a cup of sugar weigh the same as a cup of brown sugar?

1 cup of granulated sugar weighs 200 g, and 1 cup of packed brown sugar weighs 220 g.
