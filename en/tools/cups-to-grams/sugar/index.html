Sugar: cups to grams

1 cup of granulated sugar weighs 200 g (7.1 oz). Type an amount to convert.

Ingredient
SugarChange ingredient
400

2 cups sugar = 400 g

Short answer: 1 cup of granulated sugar is 200 g. 1 tablespoon is 13 g and 1 teaspoon is 4 g, calculated from the cup weight.

Source: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy, “Sugars, granulated”.

Convert grams back to cups

Divide the weight in grams by 200 to get cups of sugar. Going the other way, multiply cups by 200.

cups=grams÷200

Examples

  • 50 g sugar0.25 cups
  • 100 g sugar0.5 cups
  • 250 g sugar1.3 cups
  • 500 g sugar2.5 cups

1 pound of granulated sugar is 454 g, or 2.3 cups. 1 kilogram is 1000 g, or 5 cups of granulated sugar.

Stop converting by hand

Converting by hand gets old fast, especially when a whole recipe is in the wrong units. When you import a recipe into Matlyst, you choose how amounts are shown. With Units set to Metric in Settings, cups, ounces, and pounds from a US recipe are converted to milliliters and grams as you import it. Set it to US and Matlyst keeps the source amounts as written. The setting applies to new imports.

  • Import once, no math. Share a recipe from Instagram, TikTok, or a website, and check the draft before you save.
  • Scale the servings. Change from 1 to 20 servings and every amount scales with it. Scaling is linear, so check baking and spices yourself.
  • One grocery list. Matching items are combined and sorted by store section.

Matlyst is free to start, with 5 imports per calendar month. Read more about the recipe organizer app, or see how the grocery list works.

Questions about sugar

How many grams are in a cup of sugar?

1 cup of granulated sugar weighs 200 g (7.1 oz). Source: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy, FDC ID 169655, “Sugars, granulated”.

How many cups is 100 grams of sugar?

100 g of granulated sugar is 0.5 cups. Divide the grams by 200.

How much does 1 tablespoon of sugar weigh?

1 tablespoon of granulated sugar weighs 13 g and 1 teaspoon weighs 4 g, calculated from the cup weight.

Does brown sugar weigh the same as sugar?

No. 1 cup of packed brown sugar weighs 220 g, compared with 200 g for granulated sugar.
