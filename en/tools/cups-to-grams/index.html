Short answer: 1 cup of all-purpose flour is 125 g, 1 cup of granulated sugar is 200 g, and 1 cup of salted butter is 227 g.

About the app

Why cups don't all weigh the same

A cup measures volume, and grams measure weight. 1 cup of honey weighs 339 g, and 1 cup of rolled oats weighs 81 g. That's why each ingredient needs its own number.

Where the numbers come from

The cup weights come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy. Tablespoons and teaspoons are calculated from the cup weight (1 cup = 16 tbsp = 48 tsp). USDA gives a teaspoon weight for baking powder, baking soda, and active dry yeast, so their cup weights are calculated from that. Ounces are weight ounces (1 oz = 28.35 g), not fluid ounces.

Questions about cups to grams

How many grams are in a cup?

It depends on the ingredient. 1 cup of all-purpose flour is 125 g, 1 cup of granulated sugar is 200 g, and 1 cup of salted butter is 227 g.

How do I convert cups to grams?

Multiply the cups by the weight of 1 cup of the ingredient. 1 cup of flour weighs 125 g, so 2 cups weigh 250 g.

How do I convert grams to cups?

Divide the grams by the weight of 1 cup. 100 g of flour is 0.8 cups.

Where do the numbers come from?

From USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy. Tablespoons and teaspoons are calculated from the cup weight.
