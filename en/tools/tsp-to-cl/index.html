US teaspoons to centiliters (tsp to cl)

US teaspoons to centiliters, with a chart, the formula, and what to watch for in a recipe.

0.4929

1 tsp = 0.4929 cl

Short answer: 1 tsp = 0.4929 cl. To convert US teaspoons to centiliters, multiply by 0.4929 (or divide by 2.029).

Where recipes use US teaspoons and centiliters

US teaspoons are used in US recipes for spices, baking powder, extracts, and small amounts of anything. Centiliters are used in drinks, cocktails, and some European recipes. That is why you convert whenever a recipe is written for one system and your tools use the other.

Measuring tips

Use a liquid measuring cup for liquids and level dry measures for dry ingredients. Read a liquid measuring cup at eye level on a flat surface, and level spoons and dry measuring cups with the back of a knife. US, UK, and Australian measures with the same name are not always the same size, so check where a recipe comes from.

A quick estimate: a US cup is a little less than a quarter liter, and a liter is a little more than four cups. Use the calculator for anything you will pour into a bowl.

Common mistakes when converting

  • Using a coffee mug as a cup. A mug is rarely the size of a measuring cup, while a US cup is 236.6 ml.
  • Mixing up fluid ounces and ounces of weight. Fluid ounces measure volume, ounces on a package measure weight.
  • Assuming a US pint, quart, or gallon equals the British one. The imperial versions are about 20 percent larger.
  • Rounding too early. Convert the whole recipe first, then round the final amounts.
  • Measuring dry ingredients in a liquid measuring cup, which packs them and gives you too much.

Converting a whole recipe

When a recipe is written in US teaspoons and you cook in centiliters, convert every amount in one pass and keep the ratios the same. Write the new amounts next to the old ones so you can check your work. If you scale the recipe up or down as well, convert first and scale second, so you do not round twice.

Doing this for one recipe is fine. Doing it for a hundred saved recipes is why people use an app.

Tsp to cl questions

How many centiliters are in 1 US teaspoon?

1 US teaspoon is 0.4929 centiliters.

How many US teaspoons are in 1 centiliter?

1 centiliter is 2.029 US teaspoons.

How do I convert tsp to cl without a calculator?

Multiply by 0.4929 (or divide by 2.029). For example, 2 tsp is 0.9858 cl.

Is this conversion exact?

The factor is exact, but recipes usually round it, for example 236.6 ml for a US cup to 240 ml. Use the rounded number only when the recipe is forgiving.

What is tsp to cl used for in cooking?

US teaspoons are used in US recipes for spices, baking powder, extracts, and small amounts of anything. Centiliters are used in drinks, cocktails, and some European recipes.

Can I convert cl back to tsp?

Yes. Use the swap button in the calculator, or open the cl to tsp page. 1 centiliter is 2.029 US teaspoons.

Does Matlyst convert units in recipes?

When you import a recipe, Matlyst shows the amounts in metric or keeps the source amounts, depending on the Units setting in Settings. With Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds are converted to milliliters and grams. The setting applies to new imports.
