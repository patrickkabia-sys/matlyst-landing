Where recipes use US tablespoons and US cups

US tablespoons are used in US recipes for oil, sauces, butter, and flour. US cups are used in US recipes, which measure most ingredients by volume. That is why you convert whenever a recipe is written for one system and your tools use the other.

Measuring tips

Use a liquid measuring jug for liquids and level dry measures for dry ingredients. Read a jug at eye level on a flat surface, and level spoons and cups with the back of a knife. US, UK, and Australian measures with the same name are not always the same size, so check where a recipe comes from.

A quick estimate: a US cup is a little less than a quarter liter, and a liter is a little more than four cups. Use the calculator for anything you will pour into a bowl.

Common mistakes when converting

  • Using a coffee mug as a cup. A mug is rarely the size of a measuring cup, while a US cup is 236.6 ml.
  • Mixing up fluid ounces and ounces of weight. Fluid ounces measure volume, ounces on a package measure weight.
  • Assuming a US pint, quart, or gallon equals the British one. The imperial versions are about 20 percent larger.
  • Rounding too early. Convert the whole recipe first, then round the final amounts.
  • Measuring dry ingredients in a liquid jug, which packs them and gives you too much.

Converting a whole recipe

When a recipe is written in US tablespoons and you cook in US cups, convert every amount in one pass and keep the ratios the same. Write the new amounts next to the old ones so you can check your work. If you scale the recipe up or down as well, convert first and scale second, so you do not round twice.

Doing this for one recipe is fine. Doing it for a hundred saved recipes is why people use an app.

Stop converting by hand

Converting by hand gets old fast, especially when a whole recipe is in the wrong units. When you import a recipe into Matlyst, you choose how amounts are shown. With Units set to Metric in Settings, cups, ounces, and pounds from a US recipe are converted to milliliters and grams as you import it. Set it to US and Matlyst keeps the source amounts as written. The setting applies to new imports.

tbsp to cup questions

How many US cups are in 1 US tablespoon?

1 US tablespoon is 0.0625 US cups.

How many US tablespoons are in 1 US cup?

1 US cup is 16 US tablespoons.

How do I convert tbsp to cup without a calculator?

Divide by 16. For example, 2 tbsp is 0.125 cup.

Is this conversion exact?

The factor is exact, but recipes usually round it, for example 236.6 ml for a US cup to 240 ml. Use the rounded number only when the recipe is forgiving.

What is tbsp to cup used for in cooking?

US tablespoons are used in US recipes for oil, sauces, butter, and flour. US cups are used in US recipes, which measure most ingredients by volume.

Can I convert cup back to tbsp?

Yes. Use the swap button in the calculator, or open the cup to tbsp page. 1 US cup is 16 US tablespoons.

Does Matlyst convert units in recipes?

When you import a recipe, Matlyst shows the amounts in metric or keeps the source amounts, depending on the Units setting in Settings. With Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds are converted to milliliters and grams. The setting applies to new imports.
