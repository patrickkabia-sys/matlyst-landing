How to convert degrees Celsius to degrees Fahrenheit

The rule: multiply by 9/5, then add 32. In a formula: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.

Measuring tips

Oven dials are often inaccurate. An inexpensive oven thermometer tells you the real temperature. Fan ovens cook hotter, so many recipes suggest lowering the temperature when you use one.

A quick estimate: double the Celsius number and add 30 for a rough Fahrenheit figure. It gets less accurate as the temperature goes up, so use the calculator for baking.

Common mistakes when converting

  • Using the Celsius number on a Fahrenheit oven, which is far too cool.
  • Forgetting to lower the temperature for a fan oven.
  • Trusting the oven dial instead of a thermometer.
  • Opening the door too often, which drops the temperature fast.
  • Converting a recipe that gives a gas mark without checking your own oven.

Converting a whole recipe

When a recipe is written in degrees Celsius and you cook in degrees Fahrenheit, convert every amount in one pass and keep the ratios the same. Write the new amounts next to the old ones so you can check your work. If you scale the recipe up or down as well, convert first and scale second, so you do not round twice.

Doing this for one recipe is fine. Doing it for a hundred saved recipes is why people use an app.

°C to °F questions

How many degrees Fahrenheit are in 1 degree Celsius?

1 degree Celsius is 33.8 degrees Fahrenheit.

How many degrees Celsius are in 1 degree Fahrenheit?

1 degree Fahrenheit is -17.22 degrees Celsius.

How do I convert °C to °F without a calculator?

Multiply by 9/5, then add 32. For example, 100 °C is 212 °F.

Is this conversion exact?

The formula is exact. Recipes usually round to the nearest 5 degrees.

What is °C to °F used for in cooking?

Degrees Celsius are used in recipes and ovens outside the United States. Degrees Fahrenheit are used in US recipes and most US ovens.

Can I convert °F back to °C?

Yes. Use the swap button in the calculator, or open the °F to °C page. 1 degree Fahrenheit is -17.22 degrees Celsius.

Does Matlyst convert units in recipes?

When you import a recipe, Matlyst shows the amounts in metric or keeps the source amounts, depending on the Units setting in Settings. With Metric, cups, ounces, and pounds are converted to milliliters and grams. The setting applies to new imports.
