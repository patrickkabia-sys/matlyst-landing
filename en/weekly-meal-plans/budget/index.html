Budget meal plan

Seven dinners from ingredients that tend to be affordable, set up so little goes to waste.

Monday roast chicken, Tuesday pancakes for dinner, Wednesday chicken noodle soup with Monday’s chicken, Thursday quick and easy chili, Friday baked potatoes with Thursday’s chili, Saturday sausage and peppers, and Sunday tuna noodle casserole. Two of the dinners use leftovers from an earlier day, and the grocery list below has everything you need for the whole week.

How to use the leftovers

We list no prices, because prices change by store and by week. The week stays lean another way: the chicken and the chili are cooked once and eaten twice, and flour, eggs and milk cover several dinners.

  • Monday to Wednesday: Pick all the meat off the chicken and refrigerate half of it for Wednesday. Wednesday uses the chicken.
  • Thursday to Friday: Make a double batch of chili and refrigerate half for Friday. Friday uses the chili.

Rules for the leftovers

  • Cool food quickly. USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers must be refrigerated within 2 hours of cooking, and to cool food rapidly. “To do this, divide large amounts of food into shallow containers.”
  • Keep the refrigerator at 40 °F or below. USDA FSIS (2020) gives 40 °F or below as the safe refrigerator-storage temperature.
  • Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days. USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers can be kept in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Leftovers this week are used within two days, or frozen.
  • Reheat to 165 °F. USDA FSIS (2020) says to make sure leftovers reach 165 °F, measured with a food thermometer.
  • Freeze what you won’t eat soon. USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers can be frozen for 3 to 4 months.
  • No leftover rice or pasta. In our meal plans, rice and pasta are never saved for another day. They’re cooked fresh for each meal.

Save the week in Matlyst

Matlyst doesn’t make the meal plan for you, and the app doesn’t know what leftovers you have. What you get is a meal plan made of your own recipes, and one grocery list built from them. Here’s how to add the week:

In Matlyst

Paste a link or share a post, then check the draft before you save.

Read more
  1. Save the recipes. Open each link above and share it with Matlyst, or paste the link into the app. Read more about importing recipes.
  2. Put them on the days. Open the meal plan and put each recipe on the day it’s on here.
  3. Adjust the servings. Where the week says “cook extra,” raise the number of servings on that recipe before you shop.
  4. Add the week to the grocery list. Matlyst combines matching items into one line and groups the list by aisle (see the grocery list). Before the items are added, you see the list and can uncheck what you get from the leftovers, such as the sauce in a dinner that uses the sauce from an earlier day.

Free or Pro: The meal plan and the grocery list are free. Importing from links is free for 5 imports per calendar month, and this week has seven links. On the free plan you can save the rest the next month, or type them in yourself. With Matlyst Pro there is no such limit, and your household can share the meal plan and the grocery list.

Questions about the meal plan

Why are there no prices on the budget meal plan?

Prices change from store to store and from week to week, and we have no open source for what each dinner costs. Instead, the week is set up so you buy fewer items and throw less away.

What makes the week cheaper?

Two dinners are made from leftovers of an earlier day, and several ingredients show up in more than one dinner. Pancakes and tuna noodle casserole run on pantry basics.

How long do leftovers keep in the refrigerator?

USDA FSIS (2020) says leftovers can be kept in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Cool the food quickly and freeze what you won’t eat soon. The leftovers in this plan are used within two days.

Can I save the meal plan in Matlyst?

Yes. Save the recipes, put them on the days in the meal plan, and add the week to the grocery list. Matlyst doesn’t make the plan for you, so you add it yourself.
