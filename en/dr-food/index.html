How to save a recipe from DR Mad in Matlyst while keeping the source.

How to save the recipe

  1. Open a recipe on DR Mad.
  2. Open the share menu and choose Matlyst, or paste the link into recipe import.
  3. Review the ingredients, quantities and instructions before saving.

Source and ownership

Matlyst keeps the source link when it is available. Matlyst is not affiliated with DR Mad and does not claim ownership of its text or images.
